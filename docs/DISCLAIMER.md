## Data accuracy and completeness

- Data is collected by automated scripts from public websites and APIs.
- Records may be **incomplete, delayed, incorrectly parsed, or outdated**.
- Empty daily JSON files can occur when a source publishes nothing for that date or when the scraper fails silently.
- Always verify important information on the **original official source** before relying on it for legal, administrative, or news purposes.
